# Battery

**Battery** — no card (it and the Clock card were dropped: the bar already shows both). The bar's battery block is bright grey (`subtext1`, like the clock) and turns into a red block with pure-white text (`Theme.alertFg`) when `Power.low`. `Power.qml` owns the low-charge `notify-send -u critical`: below 20% **off mains** (gated on `onAc`, not `!charging` — a threshold hold or a weak charger must not say "plug in"), latched, re-arming above 25% or on AC. Reads `/sys/class/power_supply/BAT0` + `AC/online` every 10s (**upowerd is not running**, so `Quickshell.Services.UPower` reports nothing). **A level that sits at 80% on AC is not a stuck read:** TLP (`/etc/tlp.d/01-local.conf`, outside the repo) sets `START/STOP_CHARGE_THRESH_BAT0=75/80`, so the kernel stops charging at 80 and doesn't resume until under 75. `doas tlp fullcharge BAT0` charges to 100% once; the thresholds return on the next boot.
